Frequently asked questions

How do I win a round?

Plant five flowers of your colour in a row, column or either full diagonal. Hold that line for two seconds. Landing on an opponent’s tile clears their flowers; crossing it leaves them unchanged. Leave the tile and land there again to plant your own.

How do I use the controls?

Hold a direction to cross several tiles. Release to land. Only the landing tile changes. The capybara stops automatically at the board’s edge or when the next tile is blocked.

On iPhone and iPad, use the circular joystick on the left. Slide your thumb to change direction. Returning to the centre or releasing stops movement. Hold Walnut to repeat throws in your last direction.

On Mac, use WASD or arrow keys. Direction with Space throws a walnut; Space alone opens a leaf. On a controller, use the D-pad and A or the right trigger. Walnuts are available in clearings 3–6, leaves in 5 and 6.

How do I play on Apple TV?

Choose “Play”, set the clearing, pace and computer opponents, then choose “Let’s go!”. Practice works without internet. Directions on the remote move the capybara. Select opens a leaf; held with a direction, it throws a walnut. A controller is also supported.

Menu or Play/Pause pauses practice. During a network session, Menu ends your participation. Apple TV does not support Bluetooth rooms. Requires tvOS 17 or later.

How do clearings and the woodland journey differ?

Clearing 1 has flowers only. Other clearings feature puddles, walnuts or leaves; clearing 6 combines them all. “Woodland trail 1–6” advances to the next clearing after each round.

Five wins on a chosen clearing earn the “Friend of the forest!” title. In the journey, you need five wins on each of the six clearings.

How do we connect over Bluetooth?

This mode works on iPhone, iPad and Mac. Enable Bluetooth and grant the game access. Choose “With family” in the menu. The host chooses “Create room” and shares the code. Other players enter the code, choose “Search for hosts”, then select the discovered room.

Everyone chooses “Ready”, then the host starts with “Let’s go!”. Up to four people including the host can play, each on their own device. Keep the host app in the foreground and use compatible game versions.

Why is online play missing, or why can’t I connect?

The online option is hidden when the fixed Lemric server does not respond. Check your internet connection and try again later. If the option is visible but connecting fails, the publisher may need to correct the trusted key configuration. Players cannot change the server address or key.

Practice remains available without internet. On iPhone, iPad and Mac, you can also play over Bluetooth.

Can I play without internet?

Yes. Practice on all platforms and Bluetooth play on iPhone, iPad and Mac work without internet.

While the app is active outside a Bluetooth or online session, it checks the Lemric server approximately every 30 seconds. This also applies to Settings and help screens. An earlier request may complete after you enter a session. See the privacy policy for details.

How do I change my name, language, sound or effects?

Open Settings with the gear button. You can change your nickname, choose Polish or English and reduce flashes. Game and music volume have separate controls. Haptics can be changed on iPhone and iPad. The game saves these preferences on your device.

Do I need an account or a subscription?

The game requires no account. It has no ads, chat, subscriptions or in-app purchases. Any purchase of the app itself on the App Store is handled by Apple.

What should I include in a bug report?

Include your device, system and app versions, game mode and steps to reproduce the problem. Attach a screenshot if it helps explain the issue.

On iPhone, iPad and Mac, pause practice and choose “Export diagnostics”. Export is unavailable in network sessions and on Apple TV. The file may contain player nicknames, so inspect it before sending. The game does not send it automatically.

How do I delete data or ask about privacy?

There is no account to delete. “Your privacy choices” explains how to change permissions, remove preferences and files, and contact us about data held by the publisher.
