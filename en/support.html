FAQ

Frequently asked questions

How do I win?

Plant five flowers in your colour in a row, column or main diagonal and hold that line for two seconds. The first visit to an opponent’s tile clears their flowers; leave and return to plant your own.

How do I use the controls?

Hold a direction to cross several tiles, then release to land and mark only the final tile. On iPhone and iPad, hold the direction buttons on the left. Hold Walnut to throw in your last facing direction. On Mac, use WASD or arrow keys; direction + Space fires, Space alone opens a leaf. A game controller is supported. Leaves are available in clearings 5 and 6.

How do we connect over Bluetooth?

Enable Bluetooth and allow the app to use it in system settings. The host creates a room and shares the code; other players enter it and select the room. Everyone chooses Ready, then the host starts. Up to four people can join. Keep the host app in the foreground and use matching app versions.

Why is online play missing?

Online play is hidden when the fixed Lemric server is unavailable. Check your internet connection and try again later. The app manages the server address and trusted public key; players cannot change them. Practice and nearby Bluetooth play remain available.

Can I play without internet?

Yes. Practice and nearby Bluetooth play work without internet. When internet is available, the app automatically checks the fixed Lemric server approximately every 30 seconds in the menu and practice mode. Automatic checks do not start during Bluetooth or online sessions. See the privacy policy for details.

How do I change language, sound or effects?

Open Settings to select Polish or English, adjust sound volume, change haptics and reduce flashes. Preferences are saved on this device.

Do I need an account or a subscription?

The current game has no account, ads, chat, subscriptions or in-app purchases. Any app purchase on the App Store is handled by Apple.

What should I include in a bug report?

Include your device, OS version, app version, game mode and steps to reproduce the problem. Screenshots are helpful. Export diagnostics only if you want to; the file may contain player nicknames. Inspect it before emailing it. Never send a private server key or other secrets.

How do I delete data or report a privacy concern?

There is no account to delete. Visit Your privacy choices for local settings, exported files, permissions and requests about email correspondence or server data.
