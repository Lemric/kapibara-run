YOUR DATA

Your privacy choices

Practical steps for permissions, local data and privacy requests.

Website colour theme

Light, dark or automatic mode is saved in your browser. Clear this website’s local storage to remove the preference. Without it, the site follows your system theme.

01 Choose a nickname

Use a nickname instead of your real name. Room participants can see it. There is no account or identity profile to delete.

02 Manage local preferences

Change language, volume, haptics and visual effects in Settings. On iPhone and iPad, Delete App removes its local app data; Offload App keeps data. On Mac, removing the app bundle alone can leave preferences: contact support for help removing its local data. Device backups may contain older copies, managed through your system or backup provider.

03 Turn off Bluetooth access

End the nearby session and turn off the app’s Bluetooth permission in system settings. You can still use practice mode.

04 Control network connections

Use practice without an internet connection if you want to avoid server traffic. The app automatically checks the configured server in the menu and practice mode when a connection is available; it currently has no separate switch for these checks. Quitting the app stops them. Leaving a room ends your participation; room state may remain until closure or expiry.

05 Delete exported diagnostics

Delete files wherever you saved them, including shared or cloud locations and their recently deleted folders. Removing the app does not remove exports saved elsewhere. A recipient may hold another copy.

06 Request access or deletion

Email support for data held by the publisher, including support messages or files you sent. Describe the request and relevant date or room if needed. Do not send a password, private server key or unnecessary identity documents. If you chose another operator’s server, contact that operator too.
