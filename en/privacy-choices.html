YOUR DATA

Your privacy choices

Practical steps for permissions, local data and privacy requests.

Website colour theme

Your chosen theme is saved in the browser. Clear this website’s local storage to remove it. When you choose “Auto” or have no saved preference, the site follows your system theme.

01 Choose a nickname

Use a nickname instead of your real name. Change it in game Settings or before practice. Room participants can see it. There is no account to delete.

02 Change preferences or remove data

In game Settings, change your nickname, language, game and music volume, and reduced flashes preference. On iPhone and iPad, you can also change haptics.

On iPhone and iPad, “Delete App” removes local app data; “Offload App” keeps it. On Apple TV, delete the app together with its data. On Mac, removing only the program may leave preferences; contact support if you need help removing them.

Device backups may contain older data. Manage them through system settings or your backup provider.

03 Revoke Bluetooth permission

On iPhone, iPad or Mac, end nearby play and revoke the game’s Bluetooth permission in system settings. You can still use practice. Apple TV does not support Bluetooth rooms.

04 Control server connections

Use practice without an internet connection if you want to avoid server requests. While active outside a network session, the app also checks the server in Settings and help screens. There is no separate switch for these checks. Quitting the app stops them.

Leaving a room ends your participation. Room data may remain on the server until the expiry mechanism removes them or the server restarts.

05 Delete exported diagnostics

Export is available from paused practice on iPhone, iPad and Mac. The game does not send the file automatically and does not offer export on Apple TV.

Delete files from their saved locations, including shared folders, cloud storage and recently deleted folders. Removing the app does not remove files saved elsewhere. A recipient may keep their own copy.

06 Request access or deletion

Email support about data held by the publisher, such as correspondence and files you sent. Explain your request. Include a date or room code if helpful. Do not send passwords, private keys or identity documents that are not needed to handle the request.
