Availability and purchases

The game requires at least iOS 17 on iPhone, iPadOS 17 on iPad, macOS 14 on Mac and tvOS 17 on Apple TV. Practice works without internet. Bluetooth play requires compatible devices, game versions and permission; it works on iPhone, iPad and Mac, but not Apple TV.

The online option appears when the Lemric server responds. Connecting also requires a trusted public key configured by the publisher. The app has no subscriptions or in-app purchases. Any purchase of the game itself on the App Store, billing or refund is handled under Apple’s applicable terms.

Play considerately

Use appropriate nicknames, keep room codes within your group and respect other players. Do not use the service to harass others or send unlawful content. Do not interfere with rooms, attempt unauthorised server access or share private keys. To report a concern, email support with the time and room details if known.
