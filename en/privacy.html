01 Controller and scope

The controller for data processed by the publisher of Kapibara Run is Lemric Dominik Łabudziński. For game or privacy questions, email support@lemric.com. This policy covers the iOS, iPadOS, macOS and tvOS app, this website and communications with the publisher. The app uses the fixed Lemric game server. Players cannot change its address or trusted key.

02 Data on your device

The game saves your nickname, language, game and music volume, and reduced flashes preference on your device. On iPhone and iPad, it also saves the haptics setting. Match data and the token used to rejoin a room stay in app memory.

The server address is fixed. The publisher must configure a trusted public key in the app for server connections to work. Legacy saved server overrides are removed when this version starts.

The game requires no account. It has no ads, analytics tools or chat. It does not request access to contacts, photos, microphone or location.

03 Bluetooth and nearby play

Bluetooth play works on iPhone, iPad and Mac. Apple TV does not support this mode. Bluetooth permission allows the game to discover a host and exchange room data.

Participants exchange a room code, nicknames, device platforms, temporary identifiers, readiness and gameplay data. Your nickname is visible in the room. Choose one that does not reveal your identity. This mode does not require an internet game server.

04 Server checks and online play

While the app is active outside a Bluetooth or online session, it checks the Lemric server approximately every 30 seconds. Checks may start in the menu, practice, Settings, instructions and other help screens. A request started earlier may complete after you enter a session.

A check contains a temporary connection identifier, public key and random technical value. The server and network infrastructure see the source IP address and port. A check does not contain a nickname or match data.

During online play, the app sends the server your nickname, device platform, room code, temporary identifiers, rejoin token and player commands. The server runs the game and sends participants player information and match state. These data connect players, run matches, restore connections and limit abuse.

Gameplay transmission is encrypted and requires a trusted server public key. The availability check precedes an encrypted session; a server response alone does not confirm that the key is configured correctly.

05 Diagnostics you choose to share

On iPhone, iPad and Mac, you can pause practice and choose “Export diagnostics”. Export is unavailable during network sessions and on Apple TV.

A file may contain rules, randomisation parameters, configuration, player nicknames and identifiers, board states, events and gameplay commands. The game does not send the file automatically. You choose its location and recipient. Inspect its contents before sharing.

06 Support correspondence

When you email support, we receive your email address, message and any materials you attach, such as a bug description or diagnostics. We use them to handle your request and fix problems. This does not subscribe you to marketing. Send only data needed for your request.

07 Purposes and legal bases

Where GDPR applies, data needed to deliver requested gameplay or support are processed to perform a contract (Article 6(1)(b)).

Security, abuse prevention, technical service availability and the defence of claims rely on legitimate interests (Article 6(1)(f)). Legal obligations rely on Article 6(1)(c).

Where a processing activity requires consent, Article 6(1)(a) applies. You may withdraw consent without affecting the lawfulness of earlier processing.

08 Recipients, hosting and external services

Recipients include your room participants, the Lemric game server operator and providers of server and email infrastructure. We require providers processing data on our behalf to protect it consistently with this policy and to undertake appropriate contractual obligations. We do not sell data or share it with advertising networks.

This website is prepared for GitHub Pages hosting. Once published, GitHub processes technical visit data, including IP addresses, under its privacy statement. This website adds no cookies, analytics or tracking pixels. Fonts are hosted with the site. Your chosen colour theme is saved only in browser local storage; the site does not send the preference to the publisher.

The game server is separate from website hosting. The operator’s infrastructure may keep security logs. Contact us for provider details and actual log retention periods.

Apple handles app distribution, any payments and its own diagnostic services under Apple’s privacy policy and your device settings. Providers outside the EEA may involve transfers outside the EEA. When we arrange such transfers, we use a basis required by law, such as an adequacy decision or standard contractual clauses.

09 Retention and deletion

Preferences remain on your device until changed or the app’s data are removed. On iPhone and iPad, choose “Delete App”; “Offload App” keeps data. On Apple TV, delete the app together with its data. Removing only the program on Mac may leave preferences. Delete exported files separately from their saved locations.

The supplied game server holds rooms, nicknames, identifiers and match state in memory. It does not keep permanent match archives or accounts. Data remain until the expiry mechanism removes the room or the server restarts. Ending a match or leaving a room does not immediately delete all room state. The client rejoin token stays in session memory.

Abuse prevention uses a bounded in-memory table of IP addresses. Entries may remain for the server process lifetime. Older entries are cleaned up when the address-count threshold is exceeded; a restart removes the entire table. It is not a visit history. Infrastructure logs are separate; their retention depends on the operator.

We retain correspondence and submitted materials for the time needed to handle the request, meet legal obligations or defend claims. You may request deletion or retention details by email. Once the purpose ends, we delete or anonymise data, subject to applicable legal requirements.

10 Your choices and rights

You can use practice without internet, end a network session and revoke Bluetooth permission on iPhone, iPad or Mac. Choosing practice alone does not disable availability checks while your device has internet. The app has no separate switch for these requests. Quitting the app stops them.

You need not provide your real name or export diagnostics. See Your privacy choices for instructions.

Where applicable, you may request access, correction, erasure, restriction and portability. You may also object to processing based on legitimate interests and withdraw consent. Email us; we may request information needed to verify your entitlement to the data. A nickname alone may not uniquely identify a person.

You may complain to the competent supervisory authority; in Poland, this is the President of the Personal Data Protection Office. We do not use advertising profiling or automated decisions with legal effects on players.

11 Younger players and policy changes

The game does not require a date of birth, account or real name. Younger players should use nicknames and ask a guardian for help before sending a support request or diagnostics. This does not claim App Store Kids Category membership. If we receive unnecessary data about a child, we will take steps to remove it.

We publish the current policy at this address with its update date. Material changes to processing are described before introduction. We obtain consent where required.
