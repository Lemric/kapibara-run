Game modes

Practice

Play against one, two or three computer opponents. Choose a clearing, pace and difficulty. Practice works without internet on all supported devices.

Nearby over Bluetooth

On iPhone, iPad and Mac, the host creates a room and shares its code. A room holds up to four people including the host, each on their own device. Apple TV does not support this mode.

Online

The game uses the fixed Lemric server. The menu option appears when the server responds. Connecting also requires a trusted server key configured by the publisher.

Game rules

  1. Hold a direction to cross several tiles. Release to land. Tiles along the way stay unchanged; landing on an empty tile plants flowers.
  2. Landing on an opponent’s tile clears their flowers. Leave that tile and land there again to plant your own.
  3. Hold five of your flowers in a row, column or either full diagonal for two seconds.
  4. A walnut hit costs one energy point; a leaf blocks a hit. Crossing a puddle or losing all your energy sends you on a short picnic, then back into the game.

Choose one of six clearings or a journey through clearings 1–6. There are three speeds and three difficulty levels. Walnuts appear in clearings 3–6, puddles in 2, 4 and 6, and leaves in 5 and 6.

Controls

iPhone and iPad: play in landscape. Use the circular joystick on the left. Slide your thumb to change direction; return to the centre or release to stop. Walnut and leaf controls are on the right.

Mac: hold WASD or arrow keys. Direction with Space throws a walnut; Space alone opens a leaf. Esc pauses practice.

Apple TV: use the remote’s directions. Select opens a leaf; held with a direction, it throws a walnut. Menu or Play/Pause pauses practice.

On a controller, use the D-pad and A or the right trigger. An action with a direction throws a walnut; without a direction, it opens a leaf.

Minimum system versions: iOS 17, iPadOS 17, macOS 14 and tvOS 17.

Privacy

The game saves preferences on your device. While active outside a network session, it checks the Lemric server’s availability. Multiplayer exchanges room and gameplay data. See the privacy policy for details.
