Game modes

Practice

Play against computer opponents. Choose a clearing, pace and difficulty. Works without internet.

Nearby

Connect over Bluetooth. The host creates a room and shares the code. Up to four players can join.

Online

Play on the fixed Lemric server. Online play is available when the service is responding.

Game rules

  1. Hold a direction to cross tiles. Release to land and plant flowers only on the final tile.
  2. Landing on another player’s tile clears their flowers. Leave and land there again to plant your own.
  3. Hold five flowers in a row, column or main diagonal for two seconds.
  4. A walnut hit costs energy. A leaf blocks a hit. Puddles or empty energy mean a short picnic break.

Controls

iPhone and iPad: landscape, direction controls on the left, walnut and leaf on the right.

Mac: WASD or arrow keys. Direction + Space throws a walnut; Space alone opens a leaf.

A game controller is also supported. Leaves are available in clearings 5 and 6.

Requires iOS / iPadOS 17 or later, or macOS 14 or later.

Privacy

Preferences stay on your device. Network sessions exchange gameplay data. In the menu and practice mode, the app also checks the fixed Lemric server. See the policy for details.
