Kapibara Run

A game for iPhone, iPad and Mac. Plant five flowers in a line and hold it for two seconds to win the round.

Game rules↓Support→

No account, ads or in-app purchases.

  • 5 × 5 board
  • Up to 4 players
  • AI · Bluetooth · Online
  • Polish · English

Game modes

Practice

Play against computer opponents. Choose a clearing, pace and difficulty. Works without internet.

Nearby

Connect over Bluetooth. The host creates a room and shares the code. Up to four players can join.

Online

Play on a compatible server. This mode requires an available server and its trusted public key.

Game rules

  1. Move to adjacent tiles to plant flowers in your colour.
  2. The first visit to another player’s tile clears their flowers. Leave and return to plant your own.
  3. Hold five flowers in a row, column or main diagonal for two seconds.
  4. A walnut hit costs energy. A leaf blocks a hit. Puddles or empty energy mean a short picnic break.

Controls

iPhone and iPad: landscape, direction controls on the left, walnut and leaf on the right.

Mac: WASD or arrow keys. Direction + Space throws a walnut; Space alone opens a leaf.

A game controller is also supported. Leaves are available in clearings 5 and 6.

Requires iOS / iPadOS 17 or later, or macOS 14 or later.

Privacy

Preferences stay on your device. Network sessions exchange gameplay data. In the menu and practice mode, the app also checks the configured server. See the policy for details.
